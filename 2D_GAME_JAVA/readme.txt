Alien shooter is a game made by a computer science student Muleya Brian.
He goes by the name Penguin and is the founder and sole member of Penguin Projects (PP).
Penguin Projects was formed in 2026 when he decided to take his acquired knowledge on a test run. He started by recreating the game tasked to him in first year and from there he went on to modify it and add extra features. This game is known as an ongoing series that began as a test of how far his love for games and acquired programming knowledge can go.

The base version of the game is available in the parent folder and so will every version of the game. 

1. X-hunters (base game):
A 2D first person-player game.
The aim is to destroy all enemies. 
You move through open spaces whilst avoiding barriers. 
Destroy a enemy by stepping into their position.
Once all treasures are found, you win.
 Simple.

2. Alien Shooter (1st advanced version)
A 2D first-person player game.
The aim is to destroy all enemies.
You move through the open spaces whilst avoiding barriers.
The enemies can move in random directions at random times.
The enemies can shoot bombs that travel downward until they are out of bounds.
Getting hit by a bomb decreases your health and if enough bombs hit you - you LOSE.
As aliens are high up in the atmosphere, it is only logical to not be able to reach them you land walker and therefore, the player cannot move further past the vertical midpoint of the grid from below.
Likewise, the enemy cannot go down past the midpoint.
The player has to rely on a weapon to shoot down the alien. 
Shoot all aliens and you WIN. 
simple.
